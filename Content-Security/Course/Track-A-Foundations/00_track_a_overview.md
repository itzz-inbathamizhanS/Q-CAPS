# Track A — New Beginner / Foundations
**Entry profile:** Little or no prior exposure to quantum computing, cybersecurity, networking, cryptography, or advanced mathematics. Suitable for new college students and professionals transitioning into the field.

## Modules in this track
1. `A1` — Computing Foundations
2. `A2` — Mathematics Foundations
3. `A3` — Networking Foundations
4. `A4` — Cybersecurity Foundations
5. `A5` — Cryptography Foundations
6. `A6` — Quantum Foundations
7. `A7` — First Quantum Programming

## Beginner Capstone
Build a secure small network plus a basic cryptographic application and an introductory quantum circuit — combining A3 (networking), A5 (cryptography), and A6/A7 (quantum) into one hands-on project.

## Certificate
**CQF — Certificate in Quantum Foundations**, awarded on passing all 7 module quizzes (≥70% each) and submitting the capstone project.

## How this fits Q-CAPS
Track A is the widest on-ramp — most learners with zero background start here. It intentionally moves slowly through fundamentals before touching anything quantum-specific, so nobody is lost by the time they reach A6.
