# Track D — Enterprise Quantum Security Architect
**Entry profile:** Security architects, enterprise architects, technology leaders, security managers, senior IT professionals, and consultants who need to turn quantum risk into organizational action.

## Modules in this track
1. `E1` — Quantum Risk Management
2. `E2` — Cryptographic Discovery
3. `E3` — Quantum Readiness Assessment
4. `E4` — Crypto-Agility
5. `E5` — Enterprise PQC Migration
6. `E6` — Governance

## Architect Capstone
Produce an enterprise quantum-readiness assessment and a multi-year quantum-safe migration strategy covering assets, cryptography, network protocols, PKI, certificates, applications, vendors, governance, architecture, risks, and measurable milestones.

## Certificate
**QSA — Quantum Security Architect Certificate**, awarded on passing all 6 module quizzes (≥70% each) and submitting the capstone.
