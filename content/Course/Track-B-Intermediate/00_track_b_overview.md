# Track B — Intermediate / Quantum & Security Engineering
**Entry profile:** Learner already understands programming, computing fundamentals, and at least some networking, cybersecurity, cryptography, or quantum concepts. Placement testing may waive Track A foundation units.

## Modules in this track
1. `B1` — Advanced Mathematics for Quantum
2. `B2` — Quantum Information
3. `B3` — Quantum Algorithms
4. `B4` — Quantum Programming
5. `B5` — Quantum Hardware
6. `B6` — Network & Security Engineering
7. `B7` — Advanced Cryptography
8. `B8` — Quantum Threats
9. `B9` — PQC Fundamentals
10. `B10` — PQC Standards
11. `B11` — Intermediate PQC Labs

## Intermediate Capstone
Design and test a quantum-safe migration for a fictional organization: cryptographic inventory, vulnerable-system identification, PQC selection, hybrid deployment, and performance validation. This capstone is a smaller-scale rehearsal of the full Track D Enterprise Migration.

## Certificate
**CQSE — Certificate in Quantum Security Engineering**, awarded on passing all 11 module quizzes (≥70% each) and submitting the capstone.

## How this fits Q-CAPS
Track B is where the PQC Foundations core track (the 3-module version most Q-CAPS users complete) gets formally absorbed and extended — B8/B9/B10 revisit Shor, Grover, HNDL, and the NIST standards in much greater depth, assuming the learner already has the intuition built in Track A or the core track.
